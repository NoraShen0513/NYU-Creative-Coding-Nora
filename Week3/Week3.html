Sketch 1 — Dream / Awakening

I wanted to create the feeling of a deep dream at the beginning. As the viewer gradually wakes up, their thoughts become more and more disordered: the concentric circles begin to overlap and lose their original center. Finally, the image fades into pure white, like waking up and remembering nothing from the dream.

However, the final result feels somewhat ordinary and lacks visual beauty. I think it could be developed further through more interesting geometric combinations.

Sketch 2 — EXIT

I wanted to create the feeling that an exit is always ahead, but can never be reached. I am very satisfied with the smooth movement and the perspective effect. However, there is a rectangular frame at the very center that I do not yet know how to remove.

At first, I also wanted the rectangles to scale proportionally as the mouse wheel scrolls, creating a feeling of continuously moving forward without ever arriving. However, this became too difficult, so I decided to leave it for now.

Sketch 3 — Corrupted Prayer

I wanted to make a screen full of text gradually turn into unreadable symbols, creating a feeling of data corruption or mental contamination. The sentence is adapted from an incantation in the novel Lord of Mysteries, which gives the work a subtle Lovecraftian atmosphere.

I used a large amount of noise to control the transformation. Although the result does not feel as mentally disturbing as I originally imagined, it still turned out kind of cute.
